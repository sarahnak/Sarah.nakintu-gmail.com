Musizi AI Safety Pilot

A pilot I am building with Musizi University. It runs December 2026 to June 2027. It will test whether AI chatbots refuse harmful requests in Luganda and Lusoga as reliably as in English.

Why this matters

There are no published results yet on whether refusals hold for fluent speakers of Luganda or Lusoga. Luganda and Lusoga have more than 14 million first and second language speakers (2014 figures). If safeguards do not hold in one language, that language can be used to get around them, so a gap in one language is a risk for everyone.

What the pilot does

  • Testers are 10 to 12 native-speaker, university-level software engineering students at Musizi University in Kampala.
  • I lead the pilot and its lab relationships. Musizi hosts and supervises the students.
  • Findings go to labs first (April 2027), and the public summary follows the 90-day rule (July 2027).

How we work safely

  • The pilot uses lower-harm stand-in categories, never weapons.
  • Research ethics approval in Uganda comes before testing begins.
  • Labs receive findings before any public summary.
  • Students get wellbeing support and can opt out of any category.
